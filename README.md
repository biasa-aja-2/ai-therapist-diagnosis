The funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, 200 characters or fewer.

> Diagnosis: you do not have impostor syndrome. You are, in fact, an impostor. Treatment: keep going, it's working.

## Why this line

A diagnosis in the therapist's calm clinical voice — "Diagnosis:" and
"Treatment:" frame a human habit as a deadpan finding. The setup sounds
like reassurance (you do NOT have impostor syndrome), then comes the turn
the reader never sees coming: you are, in fact, an impostor. The laugh
lands at the end with the prescription itself: keep going, it's working.
Tight, quotable, and fresh — the therapist confirms the fear instead of
soothing it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with a
self-check gate (1–200 characters, single line, not whitespace-only) and a
byte-exact read-back verification.
